How we read the label

We read the back of the pack: the nutrition panel and the ingredient list.

We use the numbers per 100 g, as sold — not as prepared.

Ingredients are listed by weight, so the first one matters most.

If the label is missing or unclear, the product stays "Not yet rated". We never guess.

How the grade works

Every product gets a grade from A (best) to E (worst), based on its nutrition panel.

We use the same public scoring method as Europe's Nutri-Score label (2023 version).

Grades compare products within the same category — cereals with cereals, not with milk.

Drinks follow Singapore's own Nutri-Grade rules (A to D).

How claims are checked

We check the promises on the front of the pack against the label on the back.

The rules come from Singapore's official guide to nutrition labelling.

If a claim doesn't match the label, we show you both quotes side by side.

If we can't check a claim, we say so. That's an open question — never an accusation.

FAQ

Is the Hawk Grade the official Nutri-Score?

No. Hawk Grade is our own chip. We compute it with the public Nutri-Score 2023 method (Santé publique France), but we don't use the official Nutri-Score mark.

Why are some products "Not yet rated"?

The label info we need is missing or unclear. We'd rather show an honest gap than make up a grade.

Does a good grade mean a food is healthy for my child?

Grades help you pick the better option on the same shelf. They compare products within a category — they're not a judgement of your whole diet.

Where do prices come from?

FairPrice, with the date we checked shown next to them. Prices change — always check in store.

  • Halal status comes from certification only — never guessed from ingredients.
